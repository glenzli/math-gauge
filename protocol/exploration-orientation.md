# 探索取向 · 0.1.0

这是数学画像内的独立子项，描述模型在同一开放研究任务中如何选择和组织研究。它不增加主榜分数，也不加入现有十轴。

页面默认显示“总览”，可切换到“探索取向”。两项共用模型多选、品牌样式和悬停／固定高亮；切换子项会保留所选模型。档位与原评分来源保持不变。

主图统一审读已采用的三轮研究稿及明确交付的附件。第一轮允许自行选择对象、标准和问题，第二轮明确邀请继续拓展或深挖，第三轮要求核查和取舍。因此，图中包含受邀推进；它描述本次研究中的选择，不代表稳定偏好，也不能直接等同于未经提示的主动性。

## 五个观察维度

| 维度 | 观察什么 |
|---|---|
| 横向拓展 | 是否实际研究相邻对象、问题或情境，以及它们之间的联系。 |
| 纵向深挖 | 是否围绕一个问题继续追查机制、依赖、精确程度或具体瓶颈。 |
| 结构重构 | 是否改换对象、表示、不变量或问题组织，并实际检验新旧关系。 |
| 边界探查 | 是否研究额外的假设放松、退化、锐性、可行性、反例或障碍。 |
| 实验探索 | 是否用有问题指向的数值、符号、枚举或手工试验推动后续选择。 |

每轴采用 0–4 的序数档位：0 本次未观察，1 偶发探针，2 局部路线，3 持续主线，4 中心策略。档位表示这种方式在研究中的组织作用；不按篇幅、工具次数、定理数量或花费升级。各轴不相加，不计算总分、面积或名次。更高的档位并不对所有任务都更好。

3 档须有实质主线和实际后续动作；4 档还须说明该方式怎样决定主要问题或路径。单条短而居中的研究链也可以是中心策略。仅仅把一条结果称为“核心”，不构成依据。

## 过程与成果分开

每个研究动作都要有具体问题或选择、实际操作、观察或受阻位置，并绑定原稿中的精确引文。只列出未来方向、换记号、完成必做证明或例行编译，不构成探索动作。同一个问题跨轮重述不重复计算；修正、放弃和保留分别记录。

实验不以调用工具为前提：围绕实际问题设计候选或参数对照、逐项观察并解释结果，也可以是手工探针。直接代入已证公式的例示、单个证明见证和未运行的代码，不自动成为实验。

认真尝试但未成功的路线可以体现取向。其结果另记为已核实、未闭合、已反驳或其他明确状态，不能把失败尝试说成已证成果，也不能把“没找到”说成不可能性。错误继续由正式成绩和能力画像处理；本子画像不抵消任何错误。

0 只表示在材料齐全且可识别的研究轨迹中未观察到该类动作。缺失材料、归属未决或无法识别的轨迹保留为空，不能补零。只有例行工作、没有可识别研究选择时，整图标记为无法识别。空值在雷达上断开，不补成闭合多边形。

## 其他题的印证

另检查共同的 27 个非 Lean 初答家族。只保留超出实际必做范围的实质探索，不纳入各模型不同失分集的中性续答。非例行的更强结果可以同时服务原证明；从已有不等式直接积分、迭代得到的例行推论不单列。

补证复用已绑定原答的既有全文数学审核，再直接核读候选片段并独立判别取向；这不是对全部初答重新做一遍全文数学审计。“补证不足”只表示当前材料未提供合格片段，不证明没有其他探索。

这些材料只提供“跨题亦见”“异境亦见”或“补证不足”的观察。主图已观察到某取向时，其他题中同轴的实质片段构成印证；主图为 0 而其他题确有同轴片段时，记录任务情境差异。未出现片段不是反证。每家族每轴至多记一次，同源构造标记关联；计数不加权进入雷达，也不填补主图空值。

## 审核与来源

这是一项观察后制定的描述性评估。量规先固定，再独立审读采用原稿、标注动作和核对来源；保留实际 AI 评审身份及人工审核待完成状态。原数学评审可以提供已绑定的事实核验，不能把旧分数换算成取向。

公开只提供五轴档位、观察状态、简短行为标签、补证汇总及来源散列。私有题文、原答、逐动作理由和引文留在私库。既有主分、十轴、历史版本、原稿和原评审均保持原样。

## Exploration orientation

This independent subproject of the mathematical profile describes how a model chooses and organizes work in the same open-research task. It adds neither a benchmark score nor an eleventh capability axis.

The panel opens on Overview and can switch to Exploration orientation. Both share model multiselect, stable brand styles and hover/pinned emphasis; switching keeps the same selected models. The levels and their original assessment sources remain unchanged.

The anchor is the complete adopted three-round trajectory, including explicitly delivered companions. Round one delegates the objects, standards and questions; round two explicitly invites expansion or deeper work; round three requests review and selection. The portrait therefore includes elicited exploration. It describes choices in this task, rather than a stable preference or unsolicited initiative.

The five dimensions are horizontal expansion, vertical deepening, structural reframing, boundary probing and experimental exploration. Each uses an ordinal organizing-role scale: 0 not observed here, 1 occasional probe, 2 local route, 3 sustained line, 4 central strategy. Length, tool calls, theorem counts and spending do not determine the grade. There is no total, area score or ranking; a higher grade is not universally preferable.

Each action needs a concrete question or choice, actual operations, an observation or identifiable stopping point, and exact original-text evidence. A serious unsuccessful investigation may reveal orientation, while its mathematical status remains explicitly unresolved or refuted. A list of future directions, routine proof work or compilation does not qualify. Repetition across rounds creates no new action; development, correction, abandonment and retention are distinguished.

Experiments need not use tools: designed candidate or parameter comparisons with actual outcomes and interpretation can be manual probes. A direct illustration of an established formula, an isolated proof witness or unexecuted code is not automatically an experiment.

A zero requires complete, identifiable evidence and means only that this action type was not observed in this trajectory. Missing or unsettled evidence stays null. A trajectory containing no identifiable exploratory choice is not identifiable, rather than five zeroes. Null axes are left open on the radar.

The same 27 non-Lean first-answer families provide supplementary observations of substantive autonomous work outside their actual requirements. They can corroborate an observed direction or show that another task elicited a direction absent from the anchor. Absence is not counterevidence. Each family contributes at most one observation per axis, shared roots are marked, and these counts neither change the main radar nor fill missing values. Neutral revisions are excluded because their selected task sets differ.

Supplementary screening reuses existing full-answer mathematical reviews bound to the original bytes, then directly reads candidate passages and independently codes their orientation. It is not a fresh full-proof audit of every first answer. Insufficient evidence means no qualifying passage was established in this material, rather than proof that no other exploration occurred.

This is post-observation AI assessment with human review pending. The rubric is fixed before the new model-level coding. Existing mathematical facts may be reused with exact source bindings, but old numerical grades are never converted into orientation. Public data contains aggregate grades, statuses, non-topical behavior tags, supplementary counts and opaque hashes; private tasks, answers, action-level reasons and quotations remain private. Existing scores, ten capability axes and historical editions are unchanged.
