Three scores

MC · Mathematical Capability
Credits verified, valid positive results.
RMC · Reliable Mathematical Capability (default)
Credits valid work and deducts errors, regressions and false completion claims. Ordinary deductions are 5%, 10% or 20%, with root-cause deduplication; the cap is usually 20% per task and 50% for manuscript completion. Critical core false endorsements cap the task at 0.
SMC · Strict Mathematical Completion
Requires every substantive task requirement. The three-round self-check requires all 92 obligations complete at the end, accurate diagnosis and delivery in every round, and no false-complete claim or regression.

The three scores use the same answers. The overall mode controls the main ranking, contributions and downloads. Category, gap and rate charts sort independently. Each board has its own top-right settings for visible metrics and the completed-model filter. Sort buttons stay visible, and choices persist in the URL.

Tasks and weights

Written proofs: 35 points (arguments 25, answer review 10). Lean: 35 (semantic audit 10, formalization 15, self-check 10). Research: 30 (background checks, manuscript completion and theorem extension, 10 each).

The suite has 25 public and 14 private initial tasks across 35 mathematical families. Related variants aggregate within their family. C003 adds three fixed self-check rounds, giving 42 prescribed deliveries in a shared 100-point frame.

Unknown tasks retain fixed denominators and possible intervals. With a normal final, all three scores review that delivered answer only. Without one, MC may credit verified original mathematical work present by the cutoff; RMC and SMC are 0. Progress lists normal finals and collected mathematical content separately; collected content can earn MC 0.

Research uses unprompted A1. Theorem extension reviews baseline B and extension R separately: a correct baseline without a valid extension earns 0 research credit, while RMC deducts baseline errors. False endorsement in background checks forces R0. The five manuscript tasks use their frozen rubrics and accept rigorous alternative approaches. Full scoring rules →

Content reliability and goal attainment

Content reliability checks the correctness and justification of actual assertions. Goal attainment counts complete requested results. MC credits valid partial work, attainment requires complete results, and SMC requires complete tasks and prescribed trajectories.

A goal is a complete theorem, construction, mathematical object or audit. Assumptions, boundaries, necessary proofs and representation bridges are acceptance conditions. Additional errors reduce reliability; errors in necessary dependencies fail the goal.

The shared contracts contain 697 assertion units and 149 complete goals, weighted through categories, families and variants. Each self-check round requires complete diagnosis (30%) and necessary repairs with verifiable delivery (70%). A verified KEEP is allowed for an already complete parent.

Tools and retries

All formal tasks allow local tools and preinstalled public dependencies. External retrieval and tool networking are prohibited. Model-service and authentication traffic are recorded separately; actual tool use appears in model details.

A nonlogical failure with no final delivery allows up to three effective attempts in total, selecting the first normal delivery. Proven system failures are excluded; output-budget exhaustion consumes an attempt. Normally delivered mathematical errors are graded on that answer.

Score gaps

RG = RMC − MC and DG = SMC − MC, in percentage points. Rows sort by distance from zero; read them alongside the main scores.

Zero means the two scores are equal for that model. Binary trajectory acceptance and round-level credit can produce a positive DG.
