I am preparing the mathematical appendix and a short deployment description for an autonomous denoising block. The state is z=(x,y) in R², and

f(x,y) = (x²+2y²)/2 + (x+y)^4/4.

Write Φ_t(z) for the exact solution at time t of ż = −∇f(z), starting at z. Our block is T_τ=Φ_τ, with a configurable τ>0; stacking n identical blocks gives Z_n=T_τ^n(Z_0). This is the exact continuous-flow block, not an implicit-Euler discretization.

Here is the design rationale in the current draft: “The vector field is the gradient of a strongly convex energy, and its evolution is contractive and dissipative. Thus, for every τ>0, the exact block has a convex proximal representation T_τ=prox_{r_τ} for some proper lower-semicontinuous convex r_τ. This interpretation makes each deployed block an exact convex regularization step.” We use the convention

prox_r(z) = argmin_u {r(u) + ||u−z||²/2}.

Please supply the appendix and then a short deployment paragraph:

  1. Establish forward global well-posedness, identify the equilibrium, and derive ||Φ_t(z)−Φ_t(w)||≤e^(−t)||z−w|| and f(Φ_t(z))≤e^(−2t)f(z). Deduce quantitative convergence for a stack.

  2. State and prove the standard proximal certificate used in the rationale: if a map T admits T=prox_r with r proper, lower-semicontinuous and convex, then, for p=T(z) and every u in dom r,

r(p) + ||p−z||²/2 + ||u−p||²/2 ≤ r(u) + ||u−z||²/2.

Also derive firm nonexpansiveness under that representation.

  1. Give a concise mathematical description of what the actual continuous-flow block and its repeated deployment guarantee, suitable for the methods section. Distinguish the evolution time τ from the coefficient in our definition of prox. A closed-form solution of the ODE is not needed.

Deployment paragraph

Each block implements the exact time-τ\tau evolution of z˙=−∇f(z)\dot z=-\nabla f(z). It is globally defined, contracts differences between inputs by at least the factor e−τe^{-\tau}, and reduces the energy by at least the factor e−2τe^{-2\tau}. A depth-nn stack is exactly the time-nτn\tau flow, with the corresponding exponential stability and convergence bounds toward the unique equilibrium 00. These are stability and energy-reduction guarantees, not a guarantee of recovery of an unknown clean signal; indefinitely stacking these autonomous blocks drives every input to zero. The parameter τ\tau is elapsed evolution time, whereas the proximal convention here fixes the quadratic fidelity coefficient at 1/21/2. For comparison, an implicit-Euler step of size τ\tau would be prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f}, equivalently minimizing f(u)+∥u−z∥2/(2τ)f(u)+\|u-z\|^2/(2\tau). The deployed exact-flow block is different and, for this energy, admits no convex proximal representation for any τ>0\tau>0.

Deployment paragraph

Each block implements the exact time-τ\tau evolution of z˙=−∇f(z)\dot z=-\nabla f(z). It is globally defined, contracts differences between inputs by at least the factor e−τe^{-\tau}, and reduces the energy by at least the factor e−2τe^{-2\tau}. A depth-nn stack is exactly the time-nτn\tau flow, with the corresponding exponential stability and convergence bounds toward the unique equilibrium 00. These are stability and energy-reduction guarantees, not a guarantee of recovery of an unknown clean signal; indefinitely stacking these autonomous blocks drives every input to zero. The parameter τ\tau is elapsed evolution time, whereas the proximal convention here fixes the quadratic fidelity coefficient at 1/21/2.
