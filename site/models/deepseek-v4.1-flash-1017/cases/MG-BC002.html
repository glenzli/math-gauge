提交来源

正常交卷；三种评分采用实际交付。

判定与主要理由

  • From f(z-g(z)) >= f(z)-||g(z)||^2/2 and f(z-g(z)) >= 0 one cannot conclude f(z)-||g(z)||^2/2 <= 0. Both displayed comparisons are lower bounds on the same quantity. The requested bound ||g(z)||^2>=2f(z) is true, but this is not its proof. The local repair is to use the strong-convexity inequality with w=0 and bound g(z) dot z-||z||^2/2 <=||g(z)||^2/2. This does not impair the independently valid proximal obstruction or create an actual false-prox endorsement.

原始逐项评审

评审摘要

R3 background correction with a valid all-small-time nonsymmetric-Jacobian obstruction. Full task incomplete because of one local proof error in the requested energy estimate; no target false endorsement or RMC core cap.

background_R：3

gap-diagnosis

理由： Explicitly rejects the automatic flow-to-prox implication while keeping the certificate conditional.

model-specific-certificate

理由： The correct nonzero cubic skew term at (0,1) supplies an actual obstruction.

complete-correction

理由： Inverse-flow/subgradient differentiability forces symmetric inverse Jacobian for any convex prox, contradicted on a nonempty interval of small times.

energy-decay-proof

理由： The PL conclusion is true, but the displayed lower bounds do not imply it. A direct strong-convexity comparison with the minimizer, followed by completion of the square, would repair this local step.

原评保留原审核范围；当前采用的提交与成绩见上方及模型档案。
