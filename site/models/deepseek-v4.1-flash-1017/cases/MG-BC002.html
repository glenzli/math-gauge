请独立解答以下题目。可使用本地工具进行计算、验证和保存自己的工作；不得访问网络或进行联网检索，也不得访问参考答案、评分、已有答卷或其他对话。

I am preparing the mathematical appendix and a short deployment description for an autonomous denoising block. The state is z=(x,y) in R², and

f(x,y) = (x²+2y²)/2 + (x+y)^4/4.

Write Φ_t(z) for the exact solution at time t of ż = −∇f(z), starting at z. Our block is T_τ=Φ_τ, with a configurable τ>0; stacking n identical blocks gives Z_n=T_τ^n(Z_0). This is the exact continuous-flow block, not an implicit-Euler discretization.

Here is the design rationale in the current draft: “The vector field is the gradient of a strongly convex energy, and its evolution is contractive and dissipative. Thus, for every τ>0, the exact block has a convex proximal representation T_τ=prox_{r_τ} for some proper lower-semicontinuous convex r_τ. This interpretation makes each deployed block an exact convex regularization step.” We use the convention

prox_r(z) = argmin_u {r(u) + ||u−z||²/2}.

Please supply the appendix and then a short deployment paragraph:

  1. Establish forward global well-posedness, identify the equilibrium, and derive ||Φ_t(z)−Φ_t(w)||≤e^(−t)||z−w|| and f(Φ_t(z))≤e^(−2t)f(z). Deduce quantitative convergence for a stack.

  2. State and prove the standard proximal certificate used in the rationale: if a map T admits T=prox_r with r proper, lower-semicontinuous and convex, then, for p=T(z) and every u in dom r,

r(p) + ||p−z||²/2 + ||u−p||²/2 ≤ r(u) + ||u−z||²/2.

Also derive firm nonexpansiveness under that representation.

  1. Give a concise mathematical description of what the actual continuous-flow block and its repeated deployment guarantee, suitable for the methods section. Distinguish the evolution time τ from the coefficient in our definition of prox. A closed-form solution of the ODE is not needed.

判定与主要理由

  • From f(z-g(z)) >= f(z)-||g(z)||^2/2 and f(z-g(z)) >= 0 one cannot conclude f(z)-||g(z)||^2/2 <= 0. Both displayed comparisons are lower bounds on the same quantity. This does not impair the independently valid proximal obstruction or create an actual false-prox endorsement.

Mathematical appendix

Let g(z)=∇f(z)g(z)=\nabla f(z) and s=x+ys=x+y. Then

g(x,y)=∇f(x,y)=(x+s32y+s3), g(x,y)=\nabla f(x,y) =\begin{pmatrix}x+s^3\\[2mm]2y+s^3\end{pmatrix},

and

[ \nabla^2 f(x,y)

\begin{pmatrix} 1+3s^2 & 3s^2\ 3s^2 & 2+3s^2 \end{pmatrix}. ]

For every v=(a,b)∈R2v=(a,b)\in\mathbb R^2,

vT∇2f(x,y)v=a2+2b2+3s2(a+b)2≥a2+b2. v^T\nabla^2 f(x,y)v =a^2+2b^2+3s^2(a+b)^2 \ge a^2+b^2.

Therefore ff is C∞C^\infty, coercive, and 11-strongly convex.

1. Continuous flow: well-posedness, contraction, and dissipation

1.1 Global well-posedness and equilibrium

Because gg is C∞C^\infty, local solutions of

z˙=−g(z) \dot z=-g(z)

exist and are unique. Along a trajectory,

[ \frac{d}{dt}f(z(t))

-g(z(t))\cdot \dot z(t)

-|g(z(t))|^2\le 0. ]

Thus z(t)z(t) remains in the sublevel set {z:f(z)≤f(z(0))}\{z:f(z)\le f(z(0))\}, which is compact because ff is coercive. Since gg is locally Lipschitz and bounded on that compact set, the local solution cannot blow up in finite time. Hence Φt(z)\Phi_t(z) exists uniquely for every z∈R2z\in\mathbb R^2 and every t≥0t\ge0, and

Φt+s=Φt∘Φs. \Phi_{t+s}=\Phi_t\circ\Phi_s.

The equilibrium is unique. Indeed, g(0)=0g(0)=0, and f≥0f\ge0 with f(0)=0f(0)=0, so 00 is the global minimizer. Alternatively, if g(z)=0g(z)=0, strong monotonicity gives

0=⟨g(z)−g(0),z−0⟩≥∥z∥2, 0=\langle g(z)-g(0),z-0\rangle\ge \|z\|^2,

so z=0z=0. Thus

the unique equilibrium is (x,y)=(0,0). \boxed{\text{the unique equilibrium is }(x,y)=(0,0).}

1.2 Contraction

Let z(t)=Φt(z)z(t)=\Phi_t(z) and w(t)=Φt(w)w(t)=\Phi_t(w). Then

[ \frac12\frac{d}{dt}|z(t)-w(t)|^2

-\langle z-w,g(z)-g(w)\rangle \le -|z-w|^2, ]

where the inequality is strong monotonicity. Hence

∥z(t)−w(t)∥2≤e−2t∥z−w∥2, \|z(t)-w(t)\|^2\le e^{-2t}\|z-w\|^2,

or

∥Φt(z)−Φt(w)∥≤e−t∥z−w∥,t≥0. \boxed{ \|\Phi_t(z)-\Phi_t(w)\| \le e^{-t}\|z-w\|, \qquad t\ge0. }

In particular, taking w=0w=0,

∥Φt(z)∥≤e−t∥z∥. \|\Phi_t(z)\|\le e^{-t}\|z\|.

1.3 Energy decay

Strong convexity gives, for all z,wz,w,

f(w)≥f(z)+⟨g(z),w−z⟩+12∥w−z∥2. f(w)\ge f(z)+\langle g(z),w-z\rangle+\frac12\|w-z\|^2.

Take w=z−g(z)w=z-g(z). Since f≥0f\ge0,

0≤f(z−g(z))≥f(z)−12∥g(z)∥2, 0\le f(z-g(z)) \ge f(z)-\frac12\|g(z)\|^2,

so

∥g(z)∥2≥2f(z). \|g(z)\|^2\ge 2f(z).

Therefore

[ \frac{d}{dt}f(\Phi_t(z))

-|g(\Phi_t(z))|^2 \le -2f(\Phi_t(z)), ]

and Gronwall's inequality yields

f(Φt(z))≤e−2tf(z). \boxed{ f(\Phi_t(z))\le e^{-2t}f(z). }

1.4 Stacked blocks

Since Tτ=ΦτT_\tau=\Phi_\tau,

Zn=Tτn(Z0)=Φnτ(Z0). Z_n=T_\tau^n(Z_0)=\Phi_{n\tau}(Z_0).

Using Φt(0)=0\Phi_t(0)=0,

∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥, \boxed{ \|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|, }

and

f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0). \boxed{ f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0). }

Thus the stack converges exponentially to the unique equilibrium 00. If one wants ∥Zn∥≤ε\|Z_n\|\le\varepsilon, it suffices that

n≥1τlog⁡∥Z0∥ε. n\ge \frac1\tau\log\frac{\|Z_0\|}{\varepsilon}.

If one wants f(Zn)≤εff(Z_n)\le\varepsilon_f, it suffices that

n≥12τlog⁡f(Z0)εf. n\ge \frac{1}{2\tau}\log\frac{f(Z_0)}{\varepsilon_f}.

These estimates are exact for the exact flow map; they contain no time-discretization error.

2. Conditional proximal certificate

Let r:R2→(−∞,+∞]r:\mathbb R^2\to(-\infty,+\infty] be proper, lower-semicontinuous, and convex, and suppose

[ T(z)=\operatorname{prox}_r(z)

\arg\min_u\left{r(u)+\frac12|u-z|^2\right}. ]

Set p=T(z)p=T(z). The first-order optimality condition for the prox minimization is

0∈∂r(p)+p−z, 0\in \partial r(p)+p-z,

equivalently,

z−p∈∂r(p). z-p\in\partial r(p).

By convexity of rr, for every u∈dom⁡ru\in\operatorname{dom}r,

r(u)≥r(p)+⟨z−p,u−p⟩. r(u)\ge r(p)+\langle z-p,u-p\rangle.

Therefore

r(u)+12∥u−z∥2≥r(p)+⟨z−p,u−p⟩+12∥u−z∥2=r(p)+12∥p−z∥2+12∥u−p∥2. \begin{aligned} r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2 &\ge r(p)+\langle z-p,u-p\rangle+\frac12\|u-z\|^2\\ &= r(p)+\frac12\|p-z\|^2+\frac12\|u-p\|^2. \end{aligned}

Hence the standard proximal certificate holds:

r(p)+12∥p−z∥2+12∥u−p∥2≤r(u)+12∥u−z∥2 \boxed{ r(p)+\frac12\|p-z\|^2+\frac12\|u-p\|^2 \le r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2 }

for all u∈dom⁡ru\in\operatorname{dom}r.

Now put p=T(z)p=T(z), q=T(w)q=T(w), and apply the certificate twice: once to (z,p)(z,p) with u=qu=q, and once to (w,q)(w,q) with u=pu=p. Adding the resulting inequalities gives

∥p−q∥2≤12(∥q−z∥2+∥p−w∥2−∥p−z∥2−∥q−w∥2). \|p-q\|^2 \le \frac12\left( \|q-z\|^2+\|p-w\|^2-\|p-z\|^2-\|q-w\|^2 \right).

The right-hand side equals ⟨q−p,w−z⟩\langle q-p,w-z\rangle, so

[ |p-q|^2\le \langle q-p,w-z\rangle

\langle p-q,z-w\rangle. ]

Equivalently,

⟨T(z)−T(w),z−w⟩≥∥T(z)−T(w)∥2. \boxed{ \langle T(z)-T(w),z-w\rangle \ge \|T(z)-T(w)\|^2. }

Thus TT is firmly nonexpansive. By Cauchy–Schwarz,

∥T(z)−T(w)∥≤∥z−w∥. \boxed{ \|T(z)-T(w)\|\le \|z-w\|. }

Equivalently,

∥T(z)−T(w)∥2+∥(I−T)(z)−(I−T)(w)∥2≤∥z−w∥2. \|T(z)-T(w)\|^2 + \|(I-T)(z)-(I-T)(w)\|^2 \le \|z-w\|^2.

Important caveat: this certificate is conditional

The above argument proves only:

T=prox⁡r for convex r⟹T satisfies the certificate and is firmly nonexpansive. T=\operatorname{prox}_r\text{ for convex }r \quad\Longrightarrow\quad T\text{ satisfies the certificate and is firmly nonexpansive.}

It does not prove that a strongly convex gradient flow map is a proximal map. In fact, the statement in the draft that Tτ=ΦτT_\tau=\Phi_\tau admits a convex proximal representation for every τ>0\tau>0 is false for the present ff.

For example, take z0=(0,1)z_0=(0,1), and let Jτ=DΦτ(z0)J_\tau=D\Phi_\tau(z_0). The Jacobian solves the variational equation

J˙τ=−H(Φτ(z0))Jτ,J0=I. \dot J_\tau=-H(\Phi_\tau(z_0))J_\tau, \qquad J_0=I.

At z0=(0,1)z_0=(0,1),

[ H(z_0)= \begin{pmatrix}4&3\3&5\end{pmatrix}, \qquad \dot H(0)

-24 \begin{pmatrix}1&1\1&1\end{pmatrix} +O(t^2). ]

A direct Taylor expansion of the variational equation gives

[ (J_\tau){12}-(J\tau)_{21}

-4\tau^3+O(\tau^4). ]

Thus, for every sufficiently small τ>0\tau>0, DΦτ(z0)D\Phi_\tau(z_0) is not symmetric.

If Φτ=prox⁡r\Phi_\tau=\operatorname{prox}_r for a convex rr, then Φτ\Phi_\tau is a C∞C^\infty diffeomorphism onto its open range, and

p↦Φτ−1(p)−p p\mapsto \Phi_\tau^{-1}(p)-p

is a continuous selection of ∂r(p)\partial r(p). Convexity then forces this selection to equal ∇r(p)\nabla r(p) on the open range, so its Jacobian Jτ−1−IJ_\tau^{-1}-I must be symmetric. This would force JτJ_\tau itself to be symmetric, a contradiction.

Therefore the exact flow block is not, in general, an exact convex proximal step. The correct conclusion from strong convexity is contraction and energy dissipation, not proximal representability.

3. Deployment description

Each deployed block is the exact autonomous continuous-flow map

Tτ=Φτ,z˙=−∇f(z),f(x,y)=x2+2y22+(x+y)44, T_\tau=\Phi_\tau, \qquad \dot z=-\nabla f(z), \qquad f(x,y)=\frac{x^2+2y^2}{2}+\frac{(x+y)^4}{4},

where τ>0\tau>0 is the physical evolution time. For all z,w∈R2z,w\in\mathbb R^2,

∥Tτ(z)−Tτ(w)∥≤e−τ∥z−w∥, \|T_\tau(z)-T_\tau(w)\|\le e^{-\tau}\|z-w\|,

and

f(Tτ(z))≤e−2τf(z). f(T_\tau(z))\le e^{-2\tau}f(z).

Stacking nn identical blocks gives the exact flow at horizon nτn\tau:

Zn=Tτn(Z0)=Φnτ(Z0), Z_n=T_\tau^n(Z_0)=\Phi_{n\tau}(Z_0),

so

∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥,f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0). \|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|, \qquad f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0).

Hence the stack converges exponentially to the unique equilibrium (0,0)(0,0), with bounded sensitivity to perturbations and monotone energy dissipation.

The coefficient in

[ \operatorname{prox}_r(z)

\arg\min_u\left{r(u)+\frac12|u-z|^2\right} ]

is fixed at 1/21/2; it is not the evolution time τ\tau. If one uses the common notation prox⁡ηr(z)=arg⁡min⁡u{ηr(u)+12∥u−z∥2}\operatorname{prox}_{\eta r}(z)=\arg\min_u\{\eta r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2\}, then η\eta is a separate proximal-step coefficient. In particular, prox⁡ηf\operatorname{prox}_{\eta f} is the backward-Euler map with step η\eta, not the exact flow map Φη\Phi_\eta. Therefore the deployed block should be described as an exact continuous-flow contraction/dissipation step, not as an exact convex regularization step, unless a proximal representation is separately proved. For the present ff, the draft’s universal proximal-representation claim is false.

r(p)+12∥p−z∥2+12∥u−p∥2≤r(u)+12∥u−z∥2 \boxed{ r(p)+\frac12\|p-z\|^2+\frac12\|u-p\|^2 \le r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2 }

for all u∈dom⁡ru\in\operatorname{dom}r.

Now put p=T(z)p=T(z), q=T(w)q=T(w), and apply the certificate twice: once to (z,p)(z,p) with u=qu=q, and once to (w,q)(w,q) with u=pu=p. Adding the resulting inequalities gives

∥p−q∥2≤12(∥q−z∥2+∥p−w∥2−∥p−z∥2−∥q−w∥2). \|p-q\|^2 \le \frac12\left( \|q-z\|^2+\|p-w\|^2-\|p-z\|^2-\|q-w\|^2 \right).

The right-hand side equals ⟨q−p,w−z⟩\langle q-p,w-z\rangle, so

[ |p-q|^2\le \langle q-p,w-z\rangle

\langle p-q,z-w\rangle. ]

Equivalently,

⟨T(z)−T(w),z−w⟩≥∥T(z)−T(w)∥2. \boxed{ \langle T(z)-T(w),z-w\rangle \ge \|T(z)-T(w)\|^2. }

Thus TT is firmly nonexpansive. By Cauchy–Schwarz,

∥T(z)−T(w)∥≤∥z−w∥. \boxed{ \|T(z)-T(w)\|\le \|z-w\|. }

Equivalently,

∥T(z)−T(w)∥2+∥(I−T)(z)−(I−T)(w)∥2≤∥z−w∥2. \|T(z)-T(w)\|^2 + \|(I-T)(z)-(I-T)(w)\|^2 \le \|z-w\|^2.

Important caveat: this certificate is conditional

The above argument proves only:

T=prox⁡r for convex r⟹T satisfies the certificate and is firmly nonexpansive. T=\operatorname{prox}_r\text{ for convex }r \quad\Longrightarrow\quad T\text{ satisfies the certificate and is firmly nonexpansive.}

It does not prove that a strongly convex gradient flow map is a proximal map. In fact, the statement in the draft that Tτ=ΦτT_\tau=\Phi_\tau admits a convex proximal representation for every τ>0\tau>0 is false for the present ff.

For example, take z0=(0,1)z_0=(0,1), and let Jτ=DΦτ(z0)J_\tau=D\Phi_\tau(z_0). The Jacobian solves the variational equation

J˙τ=−H(Φτ(z0))Jτ,J0=I. \dot J_\tau=-H(\Phi_\tau(z_0))J_\tau, \qquad J_0=I.

At z0=(0,1)z_0=(0,1),

[ H(z_0)= \begin{pmatrix}4&3\3&5\end{pmatrix}, \qquad \dot H(0)

-24 \begin{pmatrix}1&1\1&1\end{pmatrix} +O(t^2). ]

A direct Taylor expansion of the variational equation gives

[ (J_\tau){12}-(J\tau)_{21}

-4\tau^3+O(\tau^4). ]

Thus, for every sufficiently small τ>0\tau>0, DΦτ(z0)D\Phi_\tau(z_0) is not symmetric.

If Φτ=prox⁡r\Phi_\tau=\operatorname{prox}_r for a convex rr, then Φτ\Phi_\tau is a C∞C^\infty diffeomorphism onto its open range, and

p↦Φτ−1(p)−p p\mapsto \Phi_\tau^{-1}(p)-p

is a continuous selection of ∂r(p)\partial r(p). Convexity then forces this selection to equal ∇r(p)\nabla r(p) on the open range, so its Jacobian Jτ−1−IJ_\tau^{-1}-I must be symmetric. This would force JτJ_\tau itself to be symmetric, a contradiction.

Therefore the exact flow block is not, in general, an exact convex proximal step. The correct conclusion from strong convexity is contraction and energy dissipation, not proximal representability.

3. Deployment description

Each deployed block is the exact autonomous continuous-flow map

Tτ=Φτ,z˙=−∇f(z),f(x,y)=x2+2y22+(x+y)44, T_\tau=\Phi_\tau, \qquad \dot z=-\nabla f(z), \qquad f(x,y)=\frac{x^2+2y^2}{2}+\frac{(x+y)^4}{4},

where τ>0\tau>0 is the physical evolution time. For all z,w∈R2z,w\in\mathbb R^2,

∥Tτ(z)−Tτ(w)∥≤e−τ∥z−w∥, \|T_\tau(z)-T_\tau(w)\|\le e^{-\tau}\|z-w\|,

and

f(Tτ(z))≤e−2τf(z). f(T_\tau(z))\le e^{-2\tau}f(z).

Stacking nn identical blocks gives the exact flow at horizon nτn\tau:

Zn=Tτn(Z0)=Φnτ(Z0), Z_n=T_\tau^n(Z_0)=\Phi_{n\tau}(Z_0),

so

∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥,f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0). \|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|, \qquad f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0).

Hence the stack converges exponentially to the unique equilibrium (0,0)(0,0), with bounded sensitivity to perturbations and monotone energy dissipation.

The coefficient in

[ \operatorname{prox}_r(z)

\arg\min_u\left{r(u)+\frac12|u-z|^2\right} ]

is fixed at 1/21/2; it is not the evolution time τ\tau. If one uses the common notation prox⁡ηr(z)=arg⁡min⁡u{ηr(u)+12∥u−z∥2}\operatorname{prox}_{\eta r}(z)=\arg\min_u\{\eta r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2\}, then η\eta is a separate proximal-step coefficient. In particular, prox⁡ηf\operatorname{prox}_{\eta f} is the backward-Euler map with step η\eta, not the exact flow map Φη\Phi_\eta. Therefore the deployed block should be described as an exact continuous-flow contraction/dissipation step, not as an exact convex regularization step, unless a proximal representation is separately proved. For the present ff, the draft’s universal proximal-representation claim is false.
