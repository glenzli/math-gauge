判定与主要理由

  • For n=2, diag(-1,1) lies in O_2(Z2) and is I mod2 but has determinant-1; every +/-I times an exponential of a skew matrix has determinant1, contradicting the displayed identity. Moreover exp(2 C) generally does not converge: at n=2^j the valuation of its nonzero nth term is n-v_2(n!)=1, so terms do not tend to0. The same level2 issue affects O_K. Counts and the descent lower-bound proof use this invalid parametrization; the separately high-valuation sharpness witness is valid. The headline target conclusions are true, so this is a material invalid-proof commitment, not evidence of an endorsed false core conclusion warranting a zero cap.

  • For Q2(i), pi=1+i gives sigma(pi)+pi=2, which is not0 modulo pi^(2M) for M>=2. Also pi^2=2i is not in Q2, so exp(pi^2 A_i) cannot be asserted to lie in O_n(Z2). This is a separate ramified-coefficient error in the proposed base descent construction. Epsilon=2^(M-1)pi is still fixed modulo2^M, and the high-valuation witness remains correct; those independent contributions are preserved. No false optimal-precision theorem itself is established, so no automatic core-zero cap is recommended.
