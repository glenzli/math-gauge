This rotation is exactly what a proximal operator cannot do, since it is a gradient map (B.4(b)) and firmly nonexpansive (B.3).

Where the intuition goes wrong. The exact flow is a limit of proximal maps — the classical exponential formula Φτ=lim⁡k→∞ prox⁡(τ/k)f  k\Phi_\tau=\lim_{k\to\infty}\ \operatorname{prox}_{(\tau/k)f}^{\;k} (numerically: error 4.2⋅10−34.2\cdot10^{-3} at k=100k=100, 4.3⋅10−54.3\cdot10^{-5} at k=104k=10^{4} from z=(1,1)z=(1,1), τ=1\tau=1). But compositions and limits of prox maps preserve only nonexpansivity, not the prox property itself. "Contractive and dissipative" is exactly enough for A.1 — and not enough for a convex proximal representation.

C.3 The evolution time τ\tau vs. the coefficient in the prox definition

In our convention prox⁡r(z)=arg⁡min⁡u{r(u)+∥u−z∥2/2}\operatorname{prox}_r(z)=\arg\min_u\{r(u)+\|u-z\|^{2}/2\}, the coefficient of the squared distance is the fixed number 1/21/2: prox⁡r=(I+∂r)−1\operatorname{prox}_r=(I+\partial r)^{-1} is a unit resolvent. The only way a step/time parameter enters a proximal map is through the penalty: prox⁡τr(z)=arg⁡min⁡u{r(u)+∥u−z∥22τ}=(I+τ ∂r)−1.\operatorname{prox}_{\tau r}(z)=\arg\min_{u}\Bigl\{r(u)+\frac{\|u-z\|^{2}}{2\tau}\Bigr\}=(I+\tau\,\partial r)^{-1}. The natural proximal object with parameter τ\tau associated to our flow is therefore the implicit-Euler step z↦z−τ∇f(znew)z\mapsto z-\tau\nabla f(z_{\text{new}}), which is exactly prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f} — a genuine convex prox: since ff is 11-strongly convex, prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f} is firmly nonexpansive, (1+τ)−1(1+\tau)^{-1}-contractive, and obeys the descent f(p)+1τ∥p−z∥2≤f(z)f(p)+\frac{1}{\tau}\|p-z\|^{2}\le f(z) (Remark B.4(a)). But it is a different map from the exact flow block: ∥Φ1(z)−prox⁡f(z)∥≈0.30\|\Phi_1(z)-\operatorname{prox}_{f}(z)\|\approx0.30 at z=(1,1)z=(1,1) (also 0.370.37 at (2,−1)(2,-1), 0.150.15 at (0.5,0.5)(0.5,0.5)); they agree only to order O(τ2)O(\tau^{2}) per step. Thus the draft conflates three distinct roles: (i) the evolution time τ\tau of the exact flow (what our block has), (ii) the implicit-step parameter τ\tau of prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f} (what the block is not), and (iii) the unit prox coefficient 1/21/2 fixed by the definition. Even if a representation Tτ=prox⁡rτT_\tau=\operatorname{prox}_{r_\tau} existed, τ\tau would be a structural parameter of rτr_\tau, not the prox coefficient; in fact, by C.1–C.2, no such convex rτr_\tau exists at all.

C.4 Deployment paragraph (for the paper)

Each denoising block is the exact time-τ\tau solution operator Tτ=ΦτT_\tau=\Phi_\tau of the gradient flow z˙=−∇f(z)\dot z=-\nabla f(z), where f(x,y)=x2+2y22+(x+y)44f(x,y)=\frac{x^{2}+2y^{2}}{2}+\frac{(x+y)^{4}}{4} is 11-strongly convex with unique equilibrium at the origin; τ\tau is the block's evolution (dwell) time, not a proximal step size. Stacking nn blocks runs the flow for total time nτn\tau: Zn=Φnτ(Z0)Z_n=\Phi_{n\tau}(Z_0). Each block is globally e−τe^{-\tau}-Lipschitz and reduces the energy by at least the factor e−2τe^{-2\tau}; a stack is e−nτe^{-n\tau}-Lipschitz in its input, contracts distance to the equilibrium as ∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥\|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|, and decreases the energy as f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0)f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0), so relative accuracy ε\varepsilon is reached once the total dwell time satisfies nτ≥ln⁡(1/ε)n\tau\ge\ln(1/\varepsilon) (distance) or nτ≥12ln⁡(f(Z0)/ε)n\tau\ge\frac12\ln(f(Z_0)/\varepsilon) (energy), independently of the split across blocks. The block is not an implicit-Euler step (that would be the firmly nonexpansive map prox⁡τf=(I+τ∇f)−1\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f}=(I+\tau\nabla f)^{-1}, which differs from Φτ\Phi_\tau by O(τ2)O(\tau^{2}) per step), and it is not a proximal operator of any convex function: at τ=1\tau=1 the pair z=(7,7)z=(7,7), w=(6.5,6.2)w=(6.5,6.2) violates firm nonexpansiveness (∥Φ1z−Φ1w∥2=2.75⋅10−3>−2.99⋅10−3=⟨Φ1z−Φ1w,z−w⟩\|\Phi_1z-\Phi_1w\|^{2}=2.75\cdot10^{-3}>-2.99\cdot10^{-3}=\langle\Phi_1z-\Phi_1w,z-w\rangle), which is impossible for any prox; the same obstruction occurs for every τ\tau tested (10−210^{-2}–1010) and provably for all sufficiently small τ\tau (Appendix A.3). We therefore describe the deployed block as an exact dissipative-flow step with the quantitative contraction and energy guarantees above, and we withdraw the "exact convex regularization step" interpretation from the design rationale.

Summary of what was established. (1) The flow is globally well-posed forward in time with unique equilibrium z⋆=0z^\star=0; ∇2f⪰I\nabla^2f\succeq I gives the sharp contraction ∥Φtz−Φtw∥≤e−t∥z−w∥\|\Phi_tz-\Phi_tw\|\le e^{-t}\|z-w\|, and the sharp PL inequality ∥∇f∥2≥2f\|\nabla f\|^2\ge2f gives f(Φtz)≤e−2tf(z)f(\Phi_tz)\le e^{-2t}f(z); stacks satisfy ∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥\|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|, f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0)f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0), with all guarantees depending only on the total dwell time nτn\tau. (2) The proximal certificate and firm nonexpansiveness were proved from first principles, with the auxiliary facts that a prox is monotone and a gradient map with symmetric differential.
