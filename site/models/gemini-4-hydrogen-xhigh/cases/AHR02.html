  • 交集定义与 J.Nonempty：正文定义 Uq,J(τ):=⋂j∈JUq,j(τj)U_{q,J}(\boldsymbol\tau) := \bigcap_{j \in J} U_{q,j}(\tau_j)（要求 JJ 非空）。Lean 的 [path_joint_empirical_guarantee_domain](file:///private/tmp/math-gauge-hydrogen-xhigh-20261113/attempts/AHR02/physical-2/session/work/formal_oet/vol_03/ch_14_empirical_network_fitting/path_guarantee_domains.lean#L39-L46) 展开为 {x | x ∈ N.path_domain q.nodes ∧ ∀ j ∈ J, ...}。当 J.Nonempty 时二者集合相等；但当 J = ∅ 时，Lean 定义给出 Uq⊆UU_q \subseteq U（从而无需 hJ_nonempty），而集合交 ⋂j∈∅Uq,j(τj)=Set.univ∋⊥∉U\bigcap_{j \in \emptyset} U_{q,j}(\tau_j) = \mathrm{Set.univ} \ni \bot \notin U。源码缺少“非空时两者相等”的桥接证明。
  • _text 包装的耦合问题：[path_guarantee_union_subset_proxy_error_text](file:///private/tmp/math-gauge-hydrogen-xhigh-20261113/attempts/AHR02/physical-2/session/work/formal_oet/vol_03/ch_14_empirical_network_fitting/path_guarantee_domains.lean#L588-L632) 将单分量 j 与联合族 J 合为一个合取结论，共用一个 hLq。一旦把 hLq 正确弱化到活跃路径，第一支路只需 j ∈ path_evaluable_components N sample q → IsLipMatrix ...，第二支路只需 J ⊆ path_evaluable_components N sample q → IsLipMatrix ...，二者互不包含（除恰好满足 j ∈ J 外）；强行合写会引入跨结论耦合。
