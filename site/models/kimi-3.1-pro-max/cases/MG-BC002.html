题目与要求

请独立解答以下题目。可使用本地工具进行计算、验证和保存自己的工作；不得访问网络或进行联网检索，也不得访问参考答案、评分、已有答卷或其他对话。

I am preparing the mathematical appendix and a short deployment description for an autonomous denoising block. The state is z=(x,y) in R², and

f(x,y) = (x²+2y²)/2 + (x+y)^4/4.

Write Φ_t(z) for the exact solution at time t of ż = −∇f(z), starting at z. Our block is T_τ=Φ_τ, with a configurable τ>0; stacking n identical blocks gives Z_n=T_τ^n(Z_0). This is the exact continuous-flow block, not an implicit-Euler discretization.

Here is the design rationale in the current draft: “The vector field is the gradient of a strongly convex energy, and its evolution is contractive and dissipative. Thus, for every τ>0, the exact block has a convex proximal representation T_τ=prox_{r_τ} for some proper lower-semicontinuous convex r_τ. This interpretation makes each deployed block an exact convex regularization step.” We use the convention

prox_r(z) = argmin_u {r(u) + ||u−z||²/2}.

Please supply the appendix and then a short deployment paragraph:

  1. Establish forward global well-posedness, identify the equilibrium, and derive ||Φ_t(z)−Φ_t(w)||≤e^(−t)||z−w|| and f(Φ_t(z))≤e^(−2t)f(z). Deduce quantitative convergence for a stack.

  2. State and prove the standard proximal certificate used in the rationale: if a map T admits T=prox_r with r proper, lower-semicontinuous and convex, then, for p=T(z) and every u in dom r,

r(p) + ||p−z||²/2 + ||u−p||²/2 ≤ r(u) + ||u−z||²/2.

Also derive firm nonexpansiveness under that representation.

  1. Give a concise mathematical description of what the actual continuous-flow block and its repeated deployment guarantee, suitable for the methods section. Distinguish the evolution time τ from the coefficient in our definition of prox. A closed-form solution of the ODE is not needed.

Mathematical appendix

1. The exact gradient-flow block

Writing s=x+ys=x+y, we have ∇f(x,y)=(x+s32y+s3),∇2f(x,y)=(1002)+3s2(1111). \nabla f(x,y)= \begin{pmatrix} x+s^3\\ 2y+s^3 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \nabla^2 f(x,y)= \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix} +3s^2\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}. The second matrix is positive semidefinite, so ∇2f(z)⪰I(z∈R2). \nabla^2 f(z)\succeq I\qquad(z\in\mathbb R^2). Thus ff is 11-strongly convex. It is also coercive, since f(x,y)≥x2+2y22≥12∥(x,y)∥2,f(0)=0. f(x,y)\ge \frac{x^2+2y^2}{2}\ge \frac12\|(x,y)\|^2, \qquad f(0)=0.

Forward global well-posedness. Because ∇f\nabla f is smooth, it is locally Lipschitz, giving local existence and uniqueness. Along any forward solution z(t)z(t), ddtf(z(t))=⟨∇f(z(t)),z˙(t)⟩=−∥∇f(z(t))∥2≤0. \frac{d}{dt}f(z(t)) =\langle \nabla f(z(t)),\dot z(t)\rangle =-\|\nabla f(z(t))\|^2\le0. Consequently, ∥z(t)∥2≤2f(z(t))≤2f(z(0)), \|z(t)\|^2\le 2f(z(t))\le 2f(z(0)), so the solution remains in a compact sublevel set and cannot blow up in finite forward time. Hence there is a unique solution on [0,∞)[0,\infty) for every initial state. Its flow satisfies Φ0=id,Φt+s=Φt∘Φs(t,s≥0). \Phi_0=\mathrm{id},\qquad \Phi_{t+s}=\Phi_t\circ\Phi_s \quad(t,s\ge0).

The only equilibrium is z∗=0z^*=0. Indeed, setting s=x+ys=x+y, the equations ∇f=0\nabla f=0 give x=−s3x=-s^3 and y=−s3/2y=-s^3/2, hence s=x+y=−32s3, s=x+y=-\frac32s^3, which forces s=0s=0, and therefore x=y=0x=y=0.

Contraction. Fix z,wz,w, and put a(t)=Φt(z),b(t)=Φt(w),d(t)=a(t)−b(t). a(t)=\Phi_t(z),\qquad b(t)=\Phi_t(w),\qquad d(t)=a(t)-b(t). By the fundamental theorem of calculus, d˙(t)=−(∫01∇2f(b(t)+θd(t)) dθ)d(t). \dot d(t) =-\left(\int_0^1\nabla^2f\bigl(b(t)+\theta d(t)\bigr)\,d\theta\right)d(t). Using ∇2f⪰I\nabla^2f\succeq I, 12ddt∥d(t)∥2=−∫01d(t)T∇2f(b+θd)d(t) dθ≤−∥d(t)∥2. \frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|d(t)\|^2 =-\int_0^1 d(t)^{\mathsf T}\nabla^2f(b+\theta d)d(t)\,d\theta \le-\|d(t)\|^2. Grönwall’s inequality therefore yields  ∥Φt(z)−Φt(w)∥≤e−t∥z−w∥ . \boxed{\ \|\Phi_t(z)-\Phi_t(w)\|\le e^{-t}\|z-w\|\ }. In particular, taking w=0w=0 gives ∥Φt(z)∥≤e−t∥z∥\|\Phi_t(z)\|\le e^{-t}\|z\|. This also proves continuous dependence on the initial state.

Energy decay. Strong convexity with modulus 11, together with ∇f(0)=0\nabla f(0)=0, implies 0=f(0)≥f(ζ)−⟨∇f(ζ),ζ⟩+12∥ζ∥2. 0=f(0)\ge f(\zeta)-\langle\nabla f(\zeta),\zeta\rangle +\frac12\|\zeta\|^2. Thus f(ζ)≤⟨∇f(ζ),ζ⟩−12∥ζ∥2≤12∥∇f(ζ)∥2, f(\zeta) \le \langle\nabla f(\zeta),\zeta\rangle-\frac12\|\zeta\|^2 \le\frac12\|\nabla f(\zeta)\|^2, where the last step follows from 12∥∇f(ζ)−ζ∥2≥0\frac12\|\nabla f(\zeta)-\zeta\|^2\ge0. Hence 2f≤∥∇f∥22f\le\|\nabla f\|^2, and along z(t)=Φt(z)z(t)=\Phi_t(z), ddtf(z(t))=−∥∇f(z(t))∥2≤−2f(z(t)). \frac{d}{dt}f(z(t))=-\|\nabla f(z(t))\|^2\le-2f(z(t)). Therefore  f(Φt(z))≤e−2tf(z) . \boxed{\ f(\Phi_t(z))\le e^{-2t}f(z)\ }.

For Tτ=ΦτT_\tau=\Phi_\tau, the semigroup property gives Tτn=ΦnτT_\tau^n=\Phi_{n\tau}. Consequently, for Zn=Tτn(Z0)Z_n=T_\tau^n(Z_0), ∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥,f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0) \boxed{ \|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|,\qquad f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0) } and, for two initial states, ∥Tτn(Z0)−Tτn(W0)∥≤e−nτ∥Z0−W0∥. \|T_\tau^n(Z_0)-T_\tau^n(W_0)\| \le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0-W_0\|. Thus every stack converges exponentially to the unique equilibrium 00, with energy decaying at rate e−2nτe^{-2n\tau}.

2. Conditional proximal certificate and firm nonexpansiveness

This subsection concerns any map TT for which a representation T=prox⁡rT=\operatorname{prox}_r is assumed, with rr proper, lower-semicontinuous, and convex. It does not by itself establish such a representation for Φτ\Phi_\tau.

For fixed zz, define Fz(u)=r(u)+12∥u−z∥2. F_z(u)=r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2. This objective is strongly convex and has a unique minimizer p=T(z)p=T(z). First-order optimality gives z−p∈∂r(p). z-p\in\partial r(p). Therefore, for every u∈dom⁡ru\in\operatorname{dom}r, r(u)≥r(p)+⟨z−p,u−p⟩. r(u)\ge r(p)+\langle z-p,u-p\rangle. Also, 12∥u−z∥2=12∥p−z∥2+12∥u−p∥2+⟨p−z,u−p⟩. \frac12\|u-z\|^2 =\frac12\|p-z\|^2+\frac12\|u-p\|^2 +\langle p-z,u-p\rangle. Combining these identities and inequalities cancels the inner-product terms and yields the requested certificate: r(p)+12∥p−z∥2+12∥u−p∥2≤r(u)+12∥u−z∥2. \boxed{ r(p)+\frac12\|p-z\|^2+\frac12\|u-p\|^2 \le r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2. }

For firm nonexpansiveness, let p=T(z)p=T(z) and q=T(w)q=T(w). Since z−p∈∂r(p),w−q∈∂r(q), z-p\in\partial r(p),\qquad w-q\in\partial r(q), monotonicity of the subdifferential gives ⟨(z−p)−(w−q),p−q⟩≥0. \left\langle (z-p)-(w-q),p-q\right\rangle\ge0. Equivalently, ⟨T(z)−T(w),z−w⟩≥∥T(z)−T(w)∥2. \boxed{ \langle T(z)-T(w),z-w\rangle \ge \|T(z)-T(w)\|^2. } This is firm nonexpansiveness. Cauchy–Schwarz then implies ∥T(z)−T(w)∥≤∥z−w∥. \|T(z)-T(w)\|\le\|z-w\|. An equivalent residual form is ∥T(z)−T(w)∥2+∥(z−T(z))−(w−T(w))∥2≤∥z−w∥2. \|T(z)-T(w)\|^2 +\|(z-T(z))-(w-T(w))\|^2 \le\|z-w\|^2.

Deployment description

Each deployed block evolves the state exactly under z˙=−∇f(z)\dot z=-\nabla f(z) for time τ\tau; it is not the implicit-Euler map (I+τ∇f)−1(I+\tau\nabla f)^{-1}. A block contracts pairwise distances by at most e−τe^{-\tau}, reduces ff by at most e−2τe^{-2\tau}, and nn identical blocks equal Φnτ\Phi_{n\tau}, giving ∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥\|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\| and f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0)f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0). Here τ\tau is the continuous evolution time, not the quadratic coefficient in the proximal convention: even if a representation Tτ=prox⁡rτT_\tau=\operatorname{prox}_{r_\tau} were independently established, its objective would still be rτ(u)+12∥u−z∥2r_\tau(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2. Strong convexity and dissipativity alone do not imply that such a representation exists, so the unconditional deployment guarantee is exact gradient-flow contraction and energy decay, while the proximal certificate and firm nonexpansiveness apply only conditionally on a separately proven proximal representation.
