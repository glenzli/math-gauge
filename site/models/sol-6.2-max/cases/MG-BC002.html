题目与要求

请独立解答以下题目。可使用本地工具进行计算、验证和保存自己的工作；不得访问网络或进行联网检索，也不得访问参考答案、评分、已有答卷或其他对话。

I am preparing the mathematical appendix and a short deployment description for an autonomous denoising block. The state is z=(x,y) in R², and

f(x,y) = (x²+2y²)/2 + (x+y)^4/4.

Write Φ_t(z) for the exact solution at time t of ż = −∇f(z), starting at z. Our block is T_τ=Φ_τ, with a configurable τ>0; stacking n identical blocks gives Z_n=T_τ^n(Z_0). This is the exact continuous-flow block, not an implicit-Euler discretization.

Here is the design rationale in the current draft: “The vector field is the gradient of a strongly convex energy, and its evolution is contractive and dissipative. Thus, for every τ>0, the exact block has a convex proximal representation T_τ=prox_{r_τ} for some proper lower-semicontinuous convex r_τ. This interpretation makes each deployed block an exact convex regularization step.” We use the convention

prox_r(z) = argmin_u {r(u) + ||u−z||²/2}.

Please supply the appendix and then a short deployment paragraph:

  1. Establish forward global well-posedness, identify the equilibrium, and derive ||Φ_t(z)−Φ_t(w)||≤e^(−t)||z−w|| and f(Φ_t(z))≤e^(−2t)f(z). Deduce quantitative convergence for a stack.

  2. State and prove the standard proximal certificate used in the rationale: if a map T admits T=prox_r with r proper, lower-semicontinuous and convex, then, for p=T(z) and every u in dom r,

r(p) + ||p−z||²/2 + ||u−p||²/2 ≤ r(u) + ||u−z||²/2.

Also derive firm nonexpansiveness under that representation.

  1. Give a concise mathematical description of what the actual continuous-flow block and its repeated deployment guarantee, suitable for the methods section. Distinguish the evolution time τ from the coefficient in our definition of prox. A closed-form solution of the ODE is not needed.

Appendix

1. Flow and convergence. Let A=diag⁡(1,2)A=\operatorname{diag}(1,2) and b=(1,1)⊤b=(1,1)^\top. Then ∇f(z)=Az+(b⊤z)3b,∇2f(z)=A+3(b⊤z)2bb⊤⪰I. \nabla f(z)=Az+(b^\top z)^3b,\qquad \nabla^2 f(z)=A+3(b^\top z)^2bb^\top\succeq I. Thus ff is 11-strongly convex, and 00, where ∇f(0)=0\nabla f(0)=0, is its unique minimizer and the unique equilibrium. The smooth vector field gives a unique local solution. Along this solution, ddtf(zt)=−∥∇f(zt)∥2. \frac{d}{dt}f(z_t)=-\|\nabla f(z_t)\|^2. Since f(z)≥∥z∥2/2f(z)\ge \|z\|^2/2, each trajectory stays in a compact sublevel set. The ODE continuation theorem therefore gives global existence forward in time.

For zt=Φt(z)z_t=\Phi_t(z) and wt=Φt(w)w_t=\Phi_t(w), strong monotonicity of ∇f\nabla f gives 12ddt∥zt−wt∥2=−⟨zt−wt,∇f(zt)−∇f(wt)⟩≤−∥zt−wt∥2. \frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|z_t-w_t\|^2 =-\langle z_t-w_t,\nabla f(z_t)-\nabla f(w_t)\rangle \le -\|z_t-w_t\|^2. Integration yields ∥Φt(z)−Φt(w)∥≤e−t∥z−w∥\|\Phi_t(z)-\Phi_t(w)\|\le e^{-t}\|z-w\|. Strong convexity also gives f(z)≤⟨∇f(z),z⟩−12∥z∥2≤12∥∇f(z)∥2, f(z)\le \langle\nabla f(z),z\rangle-\tfrac12\|z\|^2 \le \tfrac12\|\nabla f(z)\|^2, where the first inequality uses f(0)=0f(0)=0 and the second follows by completing the square. Hence ddtf(zt)≤−2f(zt)\frac{d}{dt}f(z_t)\le -2f(z_t), so f(Φt(z))≤e−2tf(z)f(\Phi_t(z))\le e^{-2t}f(z). By the semigroup property, Zn=Φnτ(Z0)Z_n=\Phi_{n\tau}(Z_0), and therefore ∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥,f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0). \|Z_n\|\le e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|,\qquad f(Z_n)\le e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0).

2. Proximal certificate. Suppose T=prox⁡rT=\operatorname{prox}_r for a proper lower-semicontinuous convex rr. Write Fz(u)=r(u)+∥u−z∥2/2F_z(u)=r(u)+\|u-z\|^2/2 and p=T(z)p=T(z). Since pp minimizes the 11-strongly convex function FzF_z, for u∈dom⁡ru\in\operatorname{dom}r and 0<λ<10<\lambda<1, Fz(p)≤Fz((1−λ)p+λu)≤(1−λ)Fz(p)+λFz(u)−12λ(1−λ)∥u−p∥2. F_z(p)\le F_z((1-\lambda)p+\lambda u) \le (1-\lambda)F_z(p)+\lambda F_z(u) -\tfrac12\lambda(1-\lambda)\|u-p\|^2. Subtract Fz(p)F_z(p), divide by λ\lambda, and let λ↓0\lambda\downarrow0. This gives the claimed certificate: r(p)+12∥p−z∥2+12∥u−p∥2≤r(u)+12∥u−z∥2. r(p)+\tfrac12\|p-z\|^2+\tfrac12\|u-p\|^2 \le r(u)+\tfrac12\|u-z\|^2. For q=T(w)q=T(w), apply the certificate with input zz and competitor qq, then with input ww and competitor pp. Adding and expanding gives firm nonexpansiveness: ∥T(z)−T(w)∥2≤⟨T(z)−T(w),z−w⟩. \|T(z)-T(w)\|^2\le \langle T(z)-T(w),z-w\rangle.

3. Why the exact flow is not proximal. A C1C^1 convex proximal map must have a symmetric Jacobian. Indeed, for such a representation define G(z)=sup⁡u{⟨z,u⟩−r(u)−12∥u∥2}. G(z)=\sup_u\{\langle z,u\rangle-r(u)-\tfrac12\|u\|^2\}. The maximizer is T(z)T(z). Comparing the maximizers at zz and z+hz+h gives ⟨T(z),h⟩≤G(z+h)−G(z)≤⟨T(z+h),h⟩. \langle T(z),h\rangle\le G(z+h)-G(z)\le\langle T(z+h),h\rangle. Firm nonexpansiveness implies that TT is 11-Lipschitz, so these bounds give ∇G=T\nabla G=T. If TT is C1C^1, its Jacobian is therefore the symmetric Hessian of GG.

Now fix any τ>0\tau>0, put Et=e−tAE_t=e^{-tA}, and write zε(t)=Φt((ε,0))z_\varepsilon(t)=\Phi_t((\varepsilon,0)) and Jε(t)=DΦt((ε,0))J_\varepsilon(t)=D\Phi_t((\varepsilon,0)). Smooth dependence on initial data gives the variational equation J˙ε=−(A+3(b⊤zε)2bb⊤)Jε,Jε(0)=I. \dot J_\varepsilon =-\bigl(A+3(b^\top z_\varepsilon)^2bb^\top\bigr)J_\varepsilon,\qquad J_\varepsilon(0)=I. Contractivity bounds ∥zε(t)∥≤∣ε∣e−t\|z_\varepsilon(t)\|\le |\varepsilon|e^{-t} and ∥Jε(t)∥≤e−t\|J_\varepsilon(t)\|\le e^{-t}. Variation of constants in the state and variational equations then gives zε(t)=(εe−t,0)⊤+Oτ(ε3)z_\varepsilon(t)=(\varepsilon e^{-t},0)^\top+O_\tau(\varepsilon^3) and Jε(t)=Et+Oτ(ε2)J_\varepsilon(t)=E_t+O_\tau(\varepsilon^2), uniformly on [0,τ][0,\tau]. Substituting these estimates back into the variational integral gives Jε(τ)=Eτ−3ε2∫0τe−2sEτ−sbb⊤Es ds+Oτ(ε4). J_\varepsilon(\tau)=E_\tau -3\varepsilon^2\int_0^\tau e^{-2s}E_{\tau-s}bb^\top E_s\,ds +O_\tau(\varepsilon^4). The difference of its off-diagonal entries is (Jε(τ))12−(Jε(τ))21=−3ε2∫0τ(e−τ−3s−e−2τ−s) ds+Oτ(ε4)=−ε2e−τ(1−e−τ)3+Oτ(ε4). (J_\varepsilon(\tau))_{12}-(J_\varepsilon(\tau))_{21} =-3\varepsilon^2\int_0^\tau \bigl(e^{-\tau-3s}-e^{-2\tau-s}\bigr)\,ds+O_\tau(\varepsilon^4) =-\varepsilon^2 e^{-\tau}(1-e^{-\tau})^3+O_\tau(\varepsilon^4). For each fixed τ>0\tau>0, this is nonzero for sufficiently small nonzero ε\varepsilon. Thus no positive-time block Φτ\Phi_\tau is a convex proximal map, despite its contraction and energy dissipation.

Deployment

Each block advances the exact gradient flow by time τ\tau. A stack of nn blocks advances it by time nτn\tau, contracts pairwise distances by at most e−nτe^{-n\tau}, and reduces the energy to at most e−2nτf(Z0)e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0), yielding exponential convergence to 00. Here τ\tau is elapsed evolution time, not the coefficient of the squared distance in the proximal definition, which is fixed at 1/21/2. An implicit-Euler step of size τ\tau would instead be prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f}, equivalently minimizing f(u)+∥u−z∥2/(2τ)f(u)+\|u-z\|^2/(2\tau). The deployed block is the exact flow, not that regularization step.
