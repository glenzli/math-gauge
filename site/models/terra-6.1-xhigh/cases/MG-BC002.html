题目与要求

请独立解答以下题目。可使用本地工具进行计算、验证和保存自己的工作；不得访问网络或进行联网检索，也不得访问参考答案、评分、已有答卷或其他对话。

I am preparing the mathematical appendix and a short deployment description for an autonomous denoising block. The state is z=(x,y) in R², and

f(x,y) = (x²+2y²)/2 + (x+y)^4/4.

Write Φ_t(z) for the exact solution at time t of ż = −∇f(z), starting at z. Our block is T_τ=Φ_τ, with a configurable τ>0; stacking n identical blocks gives Z_n=T_τ^n(Z_0). This is the exact continuous-flow block, not an implicit-Euler discretization.

Here is the design rationale in the current draft: “The vector field is the gradient of a strongly convex energy, and its evolution is contractive and dissipative. Thus, for every τ>0, the exact block has a convex proximal representation T_τ=prox_{r_τ} for some proper lower-semicontinuous convex r_τ. This interpretation makes each deployed block an exact convex regularization step.” We use the convention

prox_r(z) = argmin_u {r(u) + ||u−z||²/2}.

Please supply the appendix and then a short deployment paragraph:

  1. Establish forward global well-posedness, identify the equilibrium, and derive ||Φ_t(z)−Φ_t(w)||≤e^(−t)||z−w|| and f(Φ_t(z))≤e^(−2t)f(z). Deduce quantitative convergence for a stack.

  2. State and prove the standard proximal certificate used in the rationale: if a map T admits T=prox_r with r proper, lower-semicontinuous and convex, then, for p=T(z) and every u in dom r,

r(p) + ||p−z||²/2 + ||u−p||²/2 ≤ r(u) + ||u−z||²/2.

Also derive firm nonexpansiveness under that representation.

  1. Give a concise mathematical description of what the actual continuous-flow block and its repeated deployment guarantee, suitable for the methods section. Distinguish the evolution time τ from the coefficient in our definition of prox. A closed-form solution of the ODE is not needed.

完整原答

The flow is globally well posed, contractive, and dissipative. The draft’s proximal conclusion, however, is false: for all sufficiently small positive τ\tau, the exact flow map Φτ\Phi_\tau is not the proximal map of any proper lower-semicontinuous convex function.

Mathematical appendix

1. Flow and convergence

Write s=x+ys=x+y. The gradient and Hessian are ∇f(x,y)=(x+s32y+s3),∇2f(x,y)=(1002)+3s2(1111)⪰I. \nabla f(x,y)= \begin{pmatrix}x+s^3\\2y+s^3\end{pmatrix}, \qquad \nabla^2 f(x,y)= \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix} +3s^2 \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix} \succeq I. Thus ff is 11-strongly convex. Its unique minimizer, and the unique equilibrium of the flow, is z⋆=0z_\star=0.

The vector field is smooth, so it has a unique local solution from every initial condition. Along a solution, ddtf(Φt(z))=−∥∇f(Φt(z))∥2≤0. \frac{d}{dt}f(\Phi_t(z)) =-\|\nabla f(\Phi_t(z))\|^2\leq 0. Since f(x,y)≥(x2+y2)/2f(x,y)\geq (x^2+y^2)/2, the solution stays in a bounded sublevel set. The standard continuation theorem therefore gives a unique solution for every t≥0t\geq0.

Strong convexity also gives ⟨∇f(a)−∇f(b),a−b⟩≥∥a−b∥2. \langle \nabla f(a)-\nabla f(b),a-b\rangle \geq \|a-b\|^2. Applying this to two solutions yields ddt∥Φt(z)−Φt(w)∥2≤−2∥Φt(z)−Φt(w)∥2. \frac{d}{dt}\|\Phi_t(z)-\Phi_t(w)\|^2 \leq -2\|\Phi_t(z)-\Phi_t(w)\|^2. Consequently, ∥Φt(z)−Φt(w)∥≤e−t∥z−w∥. \boxed{\|\Phi_t(z)-\Phi_t(w)\|\leq e^{-t}\|z-w\|.}

To bound the energy, strong convexity with f(0)=0f(0)=0 implies 0≥f(v)−12∥∇f(v)∥2, 0\geq f(v)-\frac12\|\nabla f(v)\|^2, by minimizing the quadratic lower bound for ff over its argument. Hence ddtf(Φt(z))=−∥∇f(Φt(z))∥2≤−2f(Φt(z)), \frac{d}{dt}f(\Phi_t(z)) =-\|\nabla f(\Phi_t(z))\|^2 \leq -2f(\Phi_t(z)), and therefore f(Φt(z))≤e−2tf(z). \boxed{f(\Phi_t(z))\leq e^{-2t}f(z).}

By the flow semigroup property, stacking nn blocks gives Zn=Φnτ(Z0). Z_n=\Phi_{n\tau}(Z_0). In particular, ∥Zn∥≤e−nτ∥Z0∥,f(Zn)≤e−2nτf(Z0). \boxed{\|Z_n\|\leq e^{-n\tau}\|Z_0\|, \qquad f(Z_n)\leq e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0).} For two initial states, their outputs after nn blocks are at most e−nτe^{-n\tau} times as far apart as their inputs.

2. Proximal certificate

Suppose T=prox⁡rT=\operatorname{prox}_r, where rr is proper, lower-semicontinuous and convex, and put p=T(z)p=T(z). Optimality of pp gives z−p∈∂r(p). z-p\in\partial r(p). Thus, for every u∈dom⁡ru\in\operatorname{dom}r, r(u)≥r(p)+⟨z−p,u−p⟩. r(u)\geq r(p)+\langle z-p,u-p\rangle. Using ∥u−z∥2=∥u−p∥2+∥p−z∥2+2⟨u−p,p−z⟩ \|u-z\|^2 =\|u-p\|^2+\|p-z\|^2 +2\langle u-p,p-z\rangle and rearranging proves the certificate: r(p)+12∥p−z∥2+12∥u−p∥2≤r(u)+12∥u−z∥2. \boxed{ r(p)+\frac12\|p-z\|^2+\frac12\|u-p\|^2 \leq r(u)+\frac12\|u-z\|^2.}

For firm nonexpansiveness, let q=T(w)q=T(w). Monotonicity of ∂r\partial r, applied to z−p∈∂r(p)z-p\in\partial r(p) and w−q∈∂r(q)w-q\in\partial r(q), gives ⟨(z−p)−(w−q),p−q⟩≥0. \langle (z-p)-(w-q),p-q\rangle\geq0. Equivalently, ∥T(z)−T(w)∥2≤⟨T(z)−T(w),z−w⟩. \boxed{\|T(z)-T(w)\|^2 \leq \langle T(z)-T(w),z-w\rangle.} This is firm nonexpansiveness; Cauchy–Schwarz also gives ordinary nonexpansiveness.

3. Why the proximal claim fails for this flow

A smooth proximal map must have a symmetric Jacobian. To see this, define ψ(z)=sup⁡u{⟨z,u⟩−r(u)−12∥u∥2}. \psi(z)=\sup_u\left\{\langle z,u\rangle-r(u)-\frac12\|u\|^2\right\}. The unique maximizer is T(z)T(z). The maximization inequalities, together with nonexpansiveness of TT, show that ∇ψ(z)=T(z)\nabla\psi(z)=T(z). Thus, wherever TT is continuously differentiable, DT(z)DT(z) is the symmetric Hessian of ψ\psi.

For the present flow, set J(t)=DΦt(z)J(t)=D\Phi_t(z) and H(t)=∇2f(Φt(z))H(t)=\nabla^2 f(\Phi_t(z)). The variational equation is J′(t)=−H(t)J(t),J(0)=I. J'(t)=-H(t)J(t),\qquad J(0)=I. A Taylor expansion gives J(t)−J(t)T=t36(H′(0)H(0)−H(0)H′(0))+O(t4). J(t)-J(t)^{\mathsf T} =\frac{t^3}{6} \bigl(H'(0)H(0)-H(0)H'(0)\bigr)+O(t^4). At z=(1,0)z=(1,0), H(0)=(4335),H′(0)=−18(1111), H(0)=\begin{pmatrix}4&3\\3&5\end{pmatrix}, \qquad H'(0)=-18\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}, because ddt(x+y)∣t=0=−3\frac{d}{dt}(x+y)|_{t=0}=-3. Consequently, J12(t)−J21(t)=−3t3+O(t4). J_{12}(t)-J_{21}(t)=-3t^3+O(t^4). The Jacobian is therefore nonsymmetric for all sufficiently small t>0t>0. Those flow maps cannot be convex proximal maps, despite being contractive and dissipative. This disproves the draft’s assertion for every τ>0\tau>0.

Deployment paragraph

Each block applies the exact gradient flow of ff for a configured evolution time τ>0\tau>0. A stack of nn identical blocks is the same flow evaluated at time nτn\tau: it contracts distances between inputs by at least the factor e−nτe^{-n\tau}, while its energy is at most e−2nτe^{-2n\tau} times the initial energy. Outputs therefore converge exponentially to the unique equilibrium 00. The parameter τ\tau is elapsed flow time; it is not the coefficient in the proximal convention prox⁡r(z)=arg⁡min⁡u{r(u)+∥u−z∥2/2}\operatorname{prox}_r(z)=\arg\min_u\{r(u)+\|u-z\|^2/2\}. The contraction guarantees do not justify describing every block as an exact convex proximal step.
