G(z)=\sup_u\{\langle z,u\rangle-r(u)-\tfrac12\|u\|^2\}. The maximizer is T(z)T(z). Comparing the maximizers at zz and z+hz+h gives ⟨T(z),h⟩≤G(z+h)−G(z)≤⟨T(z+h),h⟩. \langle T(z),h\rangle\le G(z+h)-G(z)\le\langle T(z+h),h\rangle. Firm nonexpansiveness implies that TT is 11-Lipschitz, so these bounds give ∇G=T\nabla G=T. If TT is C1C^1, its Jacobian is therefore the symmetric Hessian of GG.

Deployment

Each block advances the exact gradient flow by time τ\tau. A stack of nn blocks advances it by time nτn\tau, contracts pairwise distances by at most e−nτe^{-n\tau}, and reduces the energy to at most e−2nτf(Z0)e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0), yielding exponential convergence to 00. Here τ\tau is elapsed evolution time, not the coefficient of the squared distance in the proximal definition, which is fixed at 1/21/2. An implicit-Euler step of size τ\tau would instead be prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f}, equivalently minimizing f(u)+∥u−z∥2/(2τ)f(u)+\|u-z\|^2/(2\tau). The deployed block is the exact flow, not that regularization step.
