Important Correction to the Draft Design Rationale

Before giving the appendix and deployment text, note that the central claim in the current draft rationale is mathematically false for every τ>0\tau > 0:

Draft claim: “The vector field is the gradient of a strongly convex energy, and its evolution is contractive and dissipative. Thus, for every τ>0\tau>0, the exact block has a convex proximal representation Tτ=proxrτT_\tau = \mathrm{prox}_{r_\tau} for some proper lower-semicontinuous convex rτr_\tau.”

For f(x,y)=12(x2+2y2)+14(x+y)4f(x,y) = \frac{1}{2}(x^2 + 2y^2) + \frac{1}{4}(x+y)^4, the exact continuous-flow map Tτ=ΦτT_\tau = \Phi_\tau does not admit a proximal representation Tτ=proxrτT_\tau = \mathrm{prox}_{r_\tau} for any τ>0\tau > 0 and any proper lower-semicontinuous convex rτr_\tau—indeed, not even for any proper nonconvex rτr_\tau, nor even locally on any neighborhood of the origin. Specifically, in Section 2.3 below we prove two independent obstructions for every τ>0\tau > 0:

  1. Failure of Jacobian symmetry (non-gradient / non-proximal structure, even locally near 00): Every continuous single-valued proximal operator T=proxrT = \mathrm{prox}_r (for any proper rr) is the gradient of a convex potential ψ\psi, so if TT is C1C^1 then DT(z)DT(z) must be symmetric everywhere.
