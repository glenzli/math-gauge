Deployment description

Each block advances the gradient flow of ff by time τ\tau. A stack of nn blocks implements the exact flow at time nτn\tau, contracts distances between inputs by a factor of at most e−nτe^{-n\tau}, and reduces the energy to at most e−2nτf(Z0)e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0). Every stack trajectory converges to the origin. The parameter τ\tau specifies elapsed flow time. In the stated proximal convention, the squared-distance coefficient is fixed at 1/21/2. A proximal update of ff with step size τ\tau would be prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f}, which solves the implicit-Euler equation u+τ∇f(u)=zu+\tau\nabla f(u)=z.
