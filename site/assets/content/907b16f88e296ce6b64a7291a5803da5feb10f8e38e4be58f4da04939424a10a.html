Deployment paragraph

Each block advances the exact gradient flow by elapsed time τ\tau. A stack of nn blocks advances it to time nτn\tau, contracts pairwise state distances by a factor at most e−nτe^{-n\tau}, and bounds the remaining energy by e−2nτf(Z0)e^{-2n\tau}f(Z_0). Every initial state converges to the origin. At every positive block duration, the flow violates the Jacobian symmetry required for a convex proximal representation. The proximal convention fixes the squared-distance coefficient at 1/21/2. In that convention, an implicit Euler step of size τ\tau would be prox⁡τf\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f}, equivalently the minimizer of f(u)+∥u−z∥2/(2τ)f(u)+\|u-z\|^2/(2\tau).
